Data Science · Thesis

Skill-Based Salary Prediction for Data Roles in the Philippines

Nov 2024 – Aug 2025

Undergraduate thesis, Mapúa University · Project Lead, Programmer

  • Collected Philippine data job postings from Indeed with Apify over four months (Nov 2024 – Mar 2025), then deduplicated, filtered salaries and normalized titles down to 1,057 salaried postings.
  • Used GPT-4o-mini to extract and standardize soft and technical skills from unstructured job descriptions, yielding 720 distinct skills.
  • Grouped 43 job titles into 6 role families with Sentence-BERT embeddings and K-Means, testing k with silhouette scores.
  • Compared Linear Regression, Random Forest, LightGBM and XGBoost for predicting min and max salary, with grid search and 5-fold cross-validation. XGBoost did best on the held-out test set.
  • Converted model feature importance into estimated peso values per skill to show which skills go with higher-paying roles.
